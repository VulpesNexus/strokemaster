# StrokeMaster: what to know

Both plugins address stroke defects, with different supported features and rendering routes. InDesign's draws its corrections live and saves nothing; Illustrator's writes them into the document. The [switch examples](FEATURES.md) identify which host supports each correction.

## InDesign

- **Rendered live, never saved.** Corrections are drawn on screen and into every export (PDF, PNG, EPS, PostScript, background export included). The *.indd* or *.idml* file itself is unchanged: opened where StrokeMaster is not installed (a colleague, a printer taking the package), it shows InDesign's own strokes, overlaps and spikes included. Hand over PDFs exported with the plugin.
- **Switches are global.** They apply to every open document at once and are kept on the machine, not in the document, so the same file can look different on two machines with different switches.
- **Left to InDesign:** vertical text; effects set on a stroke or fill itself (not on the object); open paths with Outside or Inside strokes, arrowheads, or dashes; separate stories that overlap, unless *Across separate stories* is on; and anything stacked between two frames of a story.
- **Trim glyph tips trims sharp tips only:** corners sharper than 60° with a curve on either side or beside an edge shorter than the stroke's reach, and corners sharper than 84° with both; beside an edge up to twice the reach, a sharp corner's miter is trimmed less, the longer the edge. Blunter corners, such as serif feet, keep their whole miters, as InDesign draws them. A center stroke reaches half as far as an outside one of the same weight, so fewer corners count as tips there: Eagle Lake's *v* keeps its pointed foot on a center stroke and loses it on an outside one.
- **Live text needs *Strokes behind other letters' fills*.** Its other corrections (miter spikes, glyph tips, Close holes) are drawn by the same pass, so they do nothing on live text with that switch off.
- **Close holes** fills cavities enclosed by compatible text strokes within a frame and across eligible neighboring frames. Separate stories participate only with *Across separate stories* enabled; intervening artwork still limits correction. Beneath a dashed or striped stroke on a shape, only the holes are filled, so the gaps between dashes stay open. See the [current verification limits](REVIEW_2026-11-02.md).
- **Different stroke widths across InDesign frames:** filled live text can share a cavity when stroke color and tint match; each width, join and miter limit is retained. Different frame linear transforms, overprinting strokes, and mixed-width hollow text are excluded. Different stroke styles within one frame still fall back to per-run hole filling. See the [follow-up verification](2026-11-02_FOLLOWUP.md).
- **Center strokes on live text:** the miters the plugin gives back are drawn after the frame's text, so where a tip reaches into a later line's letters, it shows over them, where InDesign's order would hide it.
- **Larger PDFs** from outlined decorative fonts: every corrected corner is a small shape in the PDF.
- **One build per InDesign version.** Measured on InDesign 21.5 and 21.6 (2026) on Windows; its ID prefix is a development placeholder, so a build is for your own machine.

## Illustrator

- **Mixed strokes across text frames:** when grouping would need one fill over different native strokes, *Correct Selection* keeps the frames individually corrected whenever Close holes or live-text miter correction is on, even if the current glyphs need no extra field. Existing incompatible groups are left unchanged, including their owner-set attributes; ungroup them explicitly to correct their frames separately. A cavity shared between those frames remains open. Uniform compatible strokes can share the group correction. Automatic transitions of an existing group to mixed character paint remain outside this certification.
- **Rounded tip classification:** compound paths and groups of stroked paths are treated as outlined text, including ordinary shapes with those structures; they can receive rounded tips. Other simple shapes retain the bevel treatment.

- **Saved in the document.** Corrections use Illustrator's appearance (strokes and fills in the *Appearance* panel, miter limits, dash alignment, and live effects). *Close holes inside the outline* and the optional flat-tip treatment use StrokeMaster effects: without the plugin their last results still show, but they no longer follow edits.
- **It edits your documents.** With *Correct automatically* (on by default), new and edited art is corrected as you work, in the undo step of your edit, and a switch changed re-corrects the active document at once (*Undo StrokeMaster Switches*); other open documents follow when you return to them. Undoing *Undo StrokeMaster Switches* puts the art back but not the switch: set the switch back too, or use *Correct Selection*. New art of more than 500 objects at once (a paste, for one), or a document of more than 20,000 corrected objects, is left to *Correct Selection*. With *Also documents as they open*, opening a document changes it, so Illustrator asks to save it. With *Correct automatically* off, only *Correct Selection* changes anything.
- **Structure changes.** Threaded text is grouped across its frames, and with *Across other text objects* overlapping text objects are grouped too; artwork between the frames keeps its place, so a frame with other artwork stacked between it and the rest of its story stays apart: its strokes are not merged with theirs.
- **Your hand wins.** An object whose miter limit, stroke order, or StrokeMaster stroke you change by hand is left alone from then on, also when the change was made where StrokeMaster was not running; *Correct Selection* takes it back.
- **One miter limit per path or per letter.** *No miter spikes* and *Trim glyph tips* share it, so turning one off does not bring back every miter while the other is on. Trimmed live-text tips are rounded by default; the optional flat-tip effect cuts them flat. InDesign cuts tips flush with the stroke edge.
- **Close holes** fills only beneath strokes that sit below the letters: a stroke kept above them (*Strokes added through the Appearance panel* off) gets no hole fill.
- **Dashed outside strokes become center strokes.** With *Dashed outside strokes* on, a dashed outside stroke is drawn as a center stroke at twice the weight beneath the fill, its dashes aligned to corners, and Illustrator then grays the Stroke panel's Inside and Outside for it: change its alignment with the StrokeMaster panel menu's *Align Selection* commands.
- **A research build.** Illustrator 2026 (30.x) on Windows; its name and effect identifiers (*StrokeMasterRegionProbe*, `org.strokemaster.proposed.*`) are not final.
